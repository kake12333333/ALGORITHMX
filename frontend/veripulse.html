Core Capabilities

BUILT FOR THE CRITICAL MOMENT

01
Resource Allocation

The platform helps send emergency resources where they are needed most by checking incident priority, nearby availability, and urgency. This makes response faster and avoids delays during critical situations.

02
Real-Time Alerts

Verified alerts are shared instantly with nearby volunteers and responders based on location, so the right people get informed at the right time.

03
Volunteer Network

Volunteers are matched automatically based on their skills, location, and availability, making it easier to assign help quickly during emergencies.

04
Chat

The platform includes a simple chat feature that allows users to communicate, share updates, and stay connected during emergency situations.

05
Live Incident Map

A live map shows active incidents using priority-based markers, helping users and responders quickly understand where immediate attention is needed.

06
AI Trust Scoring

Every report gets a trust score to help identify reliable alerts, reduce misinformation, and support better decisions during emergencies.

// Process Flow

FROM REPORT TO RESPONSE

01
REPORTING
Incident Reported

Users report an incident by adding basic details such as location, type of emergency, and description. The system records the information instantly so that action can begin without delay.

02
VERIFICATION
AI Evaluates & Scores

The system analyzes the report, checks its reliability, and assigns a trust score to help identify priority and reduce false alerts during emergencies.

03
RESPONSE
Volunteers Assigned

Nearby available volunteers are identified based on location and assigned quickly so that help can reach the affected area faster.

04
COMMUNICATION
Real-Time Communication

Users and volunteers can communicate through chat, share updates, and stay informed as the situation develops.

05
COMPLETION
Resolution & Rewards

Once the incident is handled, it is marked as resolved and volunteers receive points based on their participation and response.
